Design an original logo exploration board for PickMsg, an open-source ANDROID notification capture, filtering and rule automation utility. It saves incoming system notifications locally, lets users select important messages and automate actions. It is NOT a chat/social messenger, email client, AI chatbot, banking app or security antivirus. We are choosing a memorable brand symbol, not designing an interface. Create ONE coherent candidate only, not a collage of unrelated logo ideas. Square 1024x1024, flat straight-on professional identity design presentation with large calm negative space. Uniform board composition: small candidate letter and short concept name at top left; a very large standalone symbol centered in the upper-middle; the exact wordmark PickMsg directly below the hero symbol; along the bottom, three neatly aligned practical specimens of THE SAME exact symbol: an Android rounded-square launcher tile, a one-color black version on white, and a truly small-size version. The principal logo must be readable as a solid silhouette, with thick structural elements, no hairlines or tiny text. Launcher symbol stays comfortably inside the central safe area with generous padding. The candidate letter and captions are outside the logo, never baked into the launcher symbol. All variants must preserve the same geometry, NOT create a different design for each specimen. Restrained layout with small labels APP ICON, MONO, SMALL if needed. Prioritize distinctiveness and visual economy: one visual idea, no more than three substantial shapes, no generic bell icon, no app dashboard, no app screenshot, no stock mockup, no photography, no shiny 3D extrusion, no AI sparkle stars, no fake registration marks, no watermarks. Friendly rounded wordmark with subtle character, exact spelling PickMsg. The hero bird should have an iconic stout geometric profile, not a generic cartoon, suitable for a useful personal tool people enjoy keeping on their phone.

Design an original logo exploration board for PickMsg, an open-source ANDROID notification capture, filtering and rule automation utility. It saves incoming system notifications locally, lets users select important messages and automate actions. It is NOT a chat/social messenger, email client, AI chatbot, banking app or security antivirus. We are choosing a memorable brand symbol, not designing an interface. Create ONE coherent candidate only, not a collage of unrelated logo ideas. Square 1024x1024, flat straight-on professional identity design presentation with large calm negative space. Uniform board composition: small candidate letter and short concept name at top left; a very large standalone symbol centered in the upper-middle; the exact wordmark PickMsg directly below the hero symbol; along the bottom, three neatly aligned practical specimens of THE SAME exact symbol: an Android rounded-square launcher tile, a one-color black version on white, and a truly small-size version. The principal logo must be readable as a solid silhouette, with thick structural elements, no hairlines or tiny text. Launcher symbol stays comfortably inside the central safe area with generous padding. The candidate letter and captions are outside the logo, never baked into the launcher symbol. All variants must preserve the same geometry, NOT create a different design for each specimen. Restrained layout with small labels APP ICON, MONO, SMALL if needed. Prioritize distinctiveness and visual economy: one visual idea, no more than three substantial shapes, no generic bell icon, no app dashboard, no app screenshot, no stock mockup, no photography, no shiny 3D extrusion, no AI sparkle stars, no fake registration marks, no watermarks. This is a raster concept proposal in crisp flat vector-like style, not a claim to be a finished vector file.  Candidate title: A / PAPER POCKET. Direction A: quiet, architectural, tactile and trustworthy. Invent a compact folded-paper pocket that also reads subtly as a lowercase p through negative space. One broad bent ribbon forms an open receiving pocket; a single simple notification slip is nestled inside the fold. The silhouette must communicate keeping/capturing a message, not an envelope for sending mail. The p reference should emerge from the structure, not be a separate typed letter or obvious monogram pasted over a chat bubble. Rounded inner cuts balanced with one crisp diagonal paper fold. Colors deep jade #28684F and pale celadon #A8C8AD on warm ivory #F6F3EA; wordmark deep forest ink. Warm restrained humanist wordmark. The mark remains clear when rendered in one color; subtle plane separation in color is optional, but no glass gradients are needed. Launcher tile jade with ivory pocket symbol. Mature, calm and very compatible with a warm-jade app interface.
